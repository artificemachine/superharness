# NOTE — RMDI↔Perthor prospect, and what it says about superharness (2026-07-11)

A research pass (vault `07-reference/research/research-rmdi-perthor-prospect/`) digested the current RMDI stack, verified it against the LLM-routing and GPU-router markets, and assessed what RMDI offers Perthor. Two points touch superharness:

1. **The shux↔RMDI merge is the reference for "own the scaffolding, rent the intelligence" done right.** The research reaffirms the merged + deployed state (rmdi `main` on vm740 `:8200`; superharness routes delegation through `POST /dispatch/<seat>` with `(seat, bindingVersion, modelRef, recipe)` stamped into `tasks.extras_json` + the ledger; `routing_strategy` defaults to `rmdi`, `native` is the opt-out). superharness is the **task plane**; RMDI is the **model plane**. See `AUDIT-agent-harness-comparison.md` and the rmdi-side `docs/shux-merge.md`.

2. **The market check locates superharness precisely.** In RMDI's taxonomy, superharness's complexity-scored model selection (the Haiku/Sonnet/Opus ladder) is a *router* — selection policy, no binding plane — which is exactly why it was layered on top of RMDI rather than kept as a second routing brain. The LLM-routing market (LiteLLM/OpenRouter/Portkey for access; Martian/Not Diamond/RouteLLM for cost-quality selection) is the neighborhood superharness's native routing lived in; RMDI's invariant + versioned bindings + feasibility-with-evidence is the discipline that neighborhood lacks and the reason the merge dissolved the double-delegation seam (collision report C1/C2/C4).

**No action required in superharness.** This note records that the prospect research (a) confirms the merge posture is current and correct, and (b) found RMDI's enrichment to *Perthor* is pattern-transfer on the routing plane, not a component port — orthogonal to superharness's own roadmap.
