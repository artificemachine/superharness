# Incident record

## What happened
Describe the observed impact, timeline, and affected systems.

## The fix
Describe the change that resolved the incident.

## What the resolver knew
Record the evidence or context that located the cause.

## Ruled out
List hypotheses investigated and why they were excluded.

## Tracker
Link the private tracker issue (GitLab, GitHub or OpenProject) for this incident, labelled `AI-gen`, after checking that no duplicate exists. If the tracker is unreachable, record that here and keep the publication queued locally until it succeeds. Never paste a secret into the report or the issue.
